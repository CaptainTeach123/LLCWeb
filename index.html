FAQ

Good questions

How do you keep prices so low?

We use modern software, including some AI-assisted tools, to speed up routine work like first drafts, layouts and print preparation. A person directs that work and checks the results before anything reaches you, so you pay for craft and decisions rather than busywork.

Do I need to supply my own 3D model?

No. You can send a sketch, a photo, a downloaded model or just a description. We'll design or refine a model, confirm it will print well, and send you the files — or print it for you.

Can you handle a rush job for an upcoming event?

Often, yes — a streamlined process is what makes quick turnarounds possible. Tell us your date in the form and we'll say honestly whether it's realistic, especially for physical items that need printing and finishing.

Will I be able to edit my website later?

Yes. We build sites you can update yourself with simple files and plain-language instructions, and we're happy to make changes for a small fee if you'd rather not.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, so we quote each project in writing before starting. Small, well-defined jobs like a flyer or a single award design cost far less than traditional agency work. Tell us your budget and we'll shape the project to fit it.
